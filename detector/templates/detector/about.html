How it works

This project implements a convolutional neural network from first principles using only NumPy, writing every layer, gradient calculation and optimiser step by hand.

From photo to diagnosis

  1. Resize. The photo is turned landscape if needed and resized to {{ card.image_size.0 }}×{{ card.image_size.1 }} pixels, keeping the long, thin shape of a leaf.
  2. Standardise. Each colour channel is shifted and scaled with the mean and spread of the training photos.
  3. Detect patterns. Four blocks of 3×3 convolution, batch normalisation, ReLU and 2×2 max pooling learn lesion features, from edges and colour patches up to whole spots and streaks.
  4. Pool. Global average pooling measures how strongly each of 128 features appears anywhere on the leaf.
  5. Classify. A dense layer and softmax turn those 128 numbers into one probability per disease.{% if card.tta %} The photo is also scored flipped horizontally and vertically, and the three results are averaged.{% endif %}
  6. Flag. If the top probability is below {{ card.threshold|pct }}, the result is marked low confidence.

Limits

  • Trained on only 120 photos of single leaves on a plain background. Field photos with clutter are harder.
  • It knows three diseases. A healthy leaf or another disease is still assigned to one of them.
  • Brown spot and leaf smut are its most common confusion: both show small dark spots.
  • Every bacterial-leaf-blight training photo has the same resolution, which may give the model a shortcut on that class.
